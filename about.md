# About CyberSkald

CyberSkald is an independent cybersecurity research project focused on technical investigation, experimentation, and documentation.

Please note: This first rendition is for testing each page, and each page will be modified incrementally as time progresses to ensure it keeps its originality.

The project focuses on areas including:

- Malware analysis
- Vulnerability research
- Reverse engineering
- Detection engineering
- Threat research
- Security experimentation

CyberSkald is intentionally **not a cybersecurity news publication**.

Research published here is intended to document technical findings, reproduce existing research, investigate security behavior, and explore questions through experimentation.

## Research Philosophy

CyberSkald prioritizes:

- Technical research over news reporting
- Independent analysis over vendor marketing
- Reproducibility over sensationalism
- Accurate attribution over claims of novelty
- Documentation of methodology and limitations

Existing research is credited, and reproductions or extensions of previously documented techniques are identified as such.

## Research and Disclosure

Research involving vulnerabilities in third-party software will be conducted responsibly and, where appropriate, disclosed to affected parties before publication.

Malware analysis will be conducted in isolated environments, and live malware will not be distributed unnecessarily.
