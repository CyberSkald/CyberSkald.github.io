# About CyberSkald

This is an independent defensive cybersecurity research project focused on providing technical investigation, experimentation, and documentation in the form of a blog-style format.

The project intends to focus on the following areas of study:

- Malware analysis
- Vulnerability research
- Reverse engineering
- Detection engineering
- Threat research
- Security experimentation
- Whatever else I find to be worthy of discussion

CyberSkald is intentionally **not a cybersecurity news publisher**.
All information published here is **gathered from my own lab testing environment**.

- Existing research is credited, and reproductions or extensions of previously documented techniques are identified as such.
